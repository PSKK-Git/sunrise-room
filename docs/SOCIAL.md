## LinkedIn

A washer dies with a customer's bedsheets inside. The owner is at a wedding. The staff member at the counter has WhatsApp and hope.

For the Flip the Switch hackathon I put that whole scene into one Switch room: staff, owner and AI agents together. The agents investigate and prepare the refund, but nothing that costs money happens until the owner says yes.

The part I'm proudest of is the memory. I built a small agent called the scribe that never erases anything. When a rule changes, the old one is superseded and kept, so you can still explain last Tuesday's decision. And "blast" tells you what else rested on a rule before you change it.

I also got the connection code wrong four ways before reading the docs, and my own tests passed every one of them. The write-up covers that, and what is real versus pretend.
